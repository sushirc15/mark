CASINO CONCEPTS EXPLAINED

Everything you need to know about RTP, volatility, wagering requirements, and fair algorithms.

What is RTP (Return to Player) & House Edge? ▼
RTP stands for Return to Player and represents the theoretical percentage of all wagered money a slot will pay back over millions of spins. For example, a slot with a 96.50% RTP has an expected house edge of 3.50%. Higher RTP means better long-term player equity. Always check slot info before spinning!
Slot Volatility: Low, High, vs. Extreme? ▼
Volatility measures risk and win frequency. Low volatility slots hit frequent small wins. Extreme volatility slots (such as Nolimit City's San Quentin or Mental) can go hundreds of dead spins without hitting, but carry astronomical jackpot caps up to 150,000x your stake!
How do Wagering Requirements work? ▼
A wagering requirement (e.g. 35x) means you must wager the bonus amount 35 times before withdrawing. With no-wager casinos like Mr Q, all winnings from free spins are paid directly as pure withdrawable cash without any lockups!
What is Provably Fair Gaming? ▼
Provably fair technology uses cryptographic SHA-256 hashes combining a server seed, a client seed, and a nonce to prove that the casino cannot tamper with the outcome after your bet is placed.
